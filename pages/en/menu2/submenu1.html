Regulatory Overview

This product is designed and tested to meet the electrical safety, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), and radio-frequency exposure requirements of the regions where it's sold.

Testing & Certification

All compliance testing referenced below was performed by an accredited third-party testing laboratory against the specific standard cited for each certification.

Certifications & Compliance

Telecom hardware manuals almost always close their technical section with a compliance/certifications statement — plain paragraphs and a list are usually all this needs, no special CSS pattern required.

This product complies with the following standards and regulations:

  • FCC Part 15, Class B (United States)
  • CE / RED Directive 2014/53/EU (European Union)
  • Anatel — Resolução nº 242/2000 (Brazil)
  • RoHS 2 Directive 2011/65/EU — restriction of hazardous substances

Note

Changes or modifications not expressly approved by the manufacturer could void the user's authority to operate this equipment.
